**Go / no-go:**
- **M1 Core: GO.** Every video, remote-access and platform assumption holds. Required adjustments: UTC conversion, standard HLS, `available` status field, proot + a generated resolv.conf for cloudflared/tailscaled, and `webrtcAdditionalHosts` (with interface IPs on; the off-LAN tailnet WebRTC case is an M1 acceptance check).
- **M2 Motion: GO.** Keyframe-only decode costs ~3 % of one core per camera and keeps full rate with the screen off.
- **M3 Cloud: GO.** The DNS/TLS shim reaches Google and Let's Encrypt-signed hosts. rclone behaviour was already proven by `camorage.sh`.

**Other facts for M1:**
- Kernel 3.10 has no `MemAvailable`, so health uses `MemFree + Cached`.
- `net.dns1` can be IPv6; the dialer handles it.
- Budget headroom: MediaMTX + 4 readers on 1 camera = 23 % of one core and 60 MB; cloudflared adds ~8.5 % and 22 MB while streaming (MediaMTX peaked at 14.9 % / 33 MB then). Not measured: tailscaled, the portal, 3 more camera ingests. The budget is 100 % and 150 MB.
